# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This widget works entirely on your computer. It does not collect, store or send personal data to the author or to any third party, and it contains no analytics, telemetry or advertising.

**What it reads.** To show its controls it reads the state of your system: battery and power profile, screen brightness, volume and audio devices, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth status and device names, connected USB drives, KDE Connect devices, system tray items, Night Light and Do Not Disturb status, and keyboard layout. This information comes from local system services and is only shown on your screen.

**What it changes.** It changes a system setting only when you use the matching control (Do Not Disturb, Night Light, brightness, volume, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, power profile, mounting a USB drive).

**What it stores.** Only its own preferences in Plasma's configuration (hidden tray items, number of inline tray icons, 12/24-hour clock choice), plus temporary files such as tray icon copies in your session's runtime folder.

**Network.** The widget itself makes no network connections. Features such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and KDE Connect are carried out by the corresponding system services, which follow their own policies.
